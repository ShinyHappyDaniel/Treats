The core

An identity is more than color and form — it’s the feeling of a find, every time.

perks makes smart prices cheerful. We blend Scandinavian restraint — warm paper, clean typography, plenty of air — with a confident coral dot that turns every price into something to smile about.

Genuinely good value

We show the price clearly and honestly. No hidden tricks — just finds you can trust.

Scandinavian calm

Soft cream paper, generous air and a clean grotesque keep the expression grown-up and warm.

The dot speaks

The little dot is our cheerful signature — full stop, price tag and sticker in one.

04

Typography

Three families, all via Google Fonts. Bricolage Grotesque for display and wordmark, Geist for body text and UI, Geist Mono for prices, codes and labels.

Display & headings

Bricolage Grotesque

Variable, warm grotesque with optical sizing. Weight 600 with tight tracking (−0.045em) and stylistic set ss01. Carries the wordmark and all headings.
