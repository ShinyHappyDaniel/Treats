The essence

A brand identity is more than colors, type and logos. It's the personality behind everything we make.

It helps us speak with one visual voice — whether it's a website, a presentation or a sign on the wall. Used consistently, it builds recognition, trust and clarity. Kindly & Co turns everyday gifting into something warm, effortless and a little joyful.

Effortless

Gifting in seconds. Clean layouts, plain words, no friction.

Warm

Generous and human. We greet people like a friend, never a form.

Joyful

A celebratory wink in every detail — playful shapes and bright color.

01

Logotype

The logo is the most central carrier of the brand and our visual signature. Always reproduce it in its original form — give it room to breathe and let it stand clearly on its own.

Primary logo — Teal on cream or white.

Reversed white works on any brand color. Coral is the warm alternate for light, single-color contexts.

Clearspace

x x

Keep clear space equal to the height of the “k” (x) on all sides.

Minimum size

Digital — 120px wide min.

Print — 24mm wide min.

Don't

✕

Don't rotate or tilt the logo.

✕

Don't stretch or distort proportions.

✕

Don't place on low-contrast colors.

✕

Don't recolor outside the palette.
