The essence

A brand is more than colour, type and a mark. It's the feeling of a little something, on the house.

Confetti sprinkles tumble beside a soft, rounded wordmark. Treats is friendly, generous and unfussy — the brand equivalent of a smile. This document keeps that warmth consistent everywhere it shows up.

Playful

A confetti spirit. The brand never takes itself too seriously.

Warm

Friendly and human — confident without ever raising its voice.

Generous

Always a little more than expected. The brand gives before it asks.

04

A single-weight line set: 2 px strokes, rounded caps and capsule-round corners that echo the symbol. Drawn in ink on a 24 px grid, one colour only — friendly, legible and never decorative.
